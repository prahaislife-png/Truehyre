The Problem — Attack Patterns

Four attacks. All live. All hitting
remote tech hiring right now.

  • 🎭
    Proxy interviewees — A skilled person interviews; an unqualified (or non-existent) person is placed. The hiring team never meets who they hired.
  • 🤖
    Real-time deepfake video — AI face-swap overlaid live on video call. Tools are cheap, accessible, and getting better every quarter.
  • 👥
    Synthetic identities — Documents look real. The person doesn't exist — or a real person's stolen identity is being used across multiple applications.
  • 💼
    Multi-job fraud (North Korea ring) — One person holds 5–10 remote jobs simultaneously, extracting salary while installing backdoors. Amazon alone blocked 1,800+ in 2024.

Product — How It Works

Three checkpoints.
One verified identity thread.

C1
Submission Check
At candidate submission
Government ID + live selfie. Establishes a trusted identity baseline. The reference that all future checks compare against.
ID OCR LIVENESS FACE MATCH IP ANALYSIS AML (optional)
Baseline enrolled. Valid for all future submissions to any client.
C2 ★
Interview Check
Just before the live interview
Active liveness re-check. Face compared 1:1 against C1 baseline. Pool search detects duplicate identities across all candidates.
ACTIVE LIVENESS FACE MATCH 1:1 FACE SEARCH 1:N
This is what kills proxies and real-time deepfakes.
C3
Offer Check
At offer acceptance
Final biometric re-confirmation. Proves the person accepting the role is the same one who interviewed and was verified.
LIVENESS FACE MATCH 1:1
Clean legal audit trail. Documented reasonable verification.
