across three critical moments.

C1 — SUBMISSION

Government ID + live selfie. Establishes a trusted reference that all future checks compare against. Valid across all future submissions.
✓ ID genuine · Person is live · Baseline captured
C2 — INTERVIEW ★
Anti-Deepfake Check
📅 Just before the live video interview
Active liveness detection (deepfake-resistant, certified) + face comparison vs C1 + pool search for duplicate identities across all candidates.
