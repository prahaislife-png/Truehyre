The problem

Proxy interviewees, real-time deepfake video, stolen and synthetic identities — these are live attacks hitting remote tech hiring right now. A convincing fake can be created in 70 minutes with a $20 tool.


For a staffing firm, one confirmed fake placement can end a client relationship. Fraudulent hires in IT roles have installed malware on day one. The liability sits with whoever placed them.

What TrueHire is — and isn't

Is: a candidate identity and fraud verification product built for staffing. Three checkpoints across the hiring journey. Results in 2 minutes. Works in 220+ countries.


Is not: a traditional background check. No employment history, education verification, or court records in v1. We stay narrowly on the identity/anti-deepfake/AML layer where the pain is hottest and the incumbent solutions are weakest.
